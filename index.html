About Raksha Connect

Bridging the Gap Between Citizens & Emergency Services

In a moment of distress, citizens often waste precious minutes searching through conflicting phone directories, outdated blogs, or fake search ads. Raksha Connect solves this crisis by maintaining a single, authoritative directory of verified Indian emergency response systems, women helplines, cyber crime cells, and government complaint mechanisms.

Built with accessibility at its core, Raksha Connect features high-contrast rendering, screen-reader optimizations, multilingual translation, and zero login barriers.

Authoritative Source Registry:
Every record references its official nodal ministry: Ministry of Home Affairs (112.gov.in, cybercrime.gov.in), Ministry of Health (mohfw.gov.in), NHAI (nhai.gov.in), and MWCD (wcd.nic.in).
